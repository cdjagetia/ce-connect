Privacy Policy — CE Connect

Operated by P Jagetia & Associates, Chartered Accountants, Bhilwara (Rajasthan) · Last updated: 20 November 2026

CE Connect is a private client portal used only by Choudhary Enterprises and its authorised staff, and by P Jagetia & Associates, to share invoices, ledgers, documents and work requests. Access is by invitation only.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive only your name and email address to confirm that you are an authorised user. With mobile sign-in we store your mobile number and a securely hashed PIN. Documents you upload (bills, slips, papers) and messages you write are stored so that our office can act on them.

How we use it

Solely to provide accounting, tax and compliance services to Choudhary Enterprises. We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone for marketing, and we do not access any other Google data.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in the firm's Google Workspace (Google Drive and Google Sheets) with access restricted to authorised persons. Every sign-in and file access is logged.

Your choices

You may ask us to deactivate your access or delete your user record at any time.
